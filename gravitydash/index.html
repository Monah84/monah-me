Gravity Dash

One tap flips gravity. That is the whole control. You are pulled to the floor, then to the ceiling, and back again, and your job is to find the gap.

It is built for short sessions. A run takes about a minute, there is nothing to sign up for, and it works with no connection at all.

Pick a difficulty that suits you

GentleStarts slow, keeps the gaps wide, and gives you two shields to spend on mistakes.
NormalThe standard run. One shield.
HardNo shields at all. Nothing between you and the wall.

The speed builds gradually in every mode rather than starting fast, so there is room to learn the flip before the game asks anything of you.

Four ways to play

ClassicEndless. Survive as long as you can.
Speed RunSixty seconds, faster from the first obstacle.
Perfect RunNo shields, no power-ups. One hit ends it.
DailyOne fixed layout, the same for everybody, every day.

Power-ups

Shield absorbs one hit. Magnet pulls in nearby stars. Slow-Mo gives you a moment to think.

What it does not do

No account. No sign-up. No chat, and no way for anyone to contact you through the game. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The detail is in the privacy policy, which is short.

Get in touch
