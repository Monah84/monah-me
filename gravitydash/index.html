Gravity Dash

One tap flips which way gravity pulls. Fly through the gap in each obstacle, collect the stars, and see how far you get.

There are three difficulties. Gentle starts slow, keeps the gaps wide and gives you two shields. Hard gives you none. The speed builds gradually rather than starting fast, so there is room to learn the flip before it asks anything of you.
