Privacy Policy

Gravity Dash · last updated 27 August 2026

The short version. We do not collect your name, your email, or anything that identifies you. There is no account and no sign-in. Your scores and settings stay on your device.

What the game saves on the device

Your high score, your chosen difficulty, whether sound is muted, and anything you have bought. All of it stays on the device and is removed when you delete the app.

Adverts

The game shows a banner advert, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use a device identifier to deliver an advert, count it, and detect fraud. Google explains its side at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Buying Remove Ads hides the banner permanently.

Purchases

Two things can be bought: Remove Ads, and a pack of continues that revive a run. Both are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details, and nothing about a purchase is sent to us.

There are no donations in this app. If you would like to support the work, that happens outside the app, not through it.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your score is submitted to Apple's leaderboards under whatever nickname you have chosen there. That is between you and Apple; we never see your Game Center account. Declining Game Center costs you nothing but the leaderboard.

Your rights

We hold no account and no personal data, so there is nothing for us to look up, export, or delete. What the game knows is on your device and under your control.
